Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Instant Mobile Utilities apps listed on our apps page, including Trip Expense Manager, Universal Scanner, Share My Location, and AI Media Manager.

Information we collect

Trip Expense Manager sends the expense information and receipt images you add to our servers so the app can provide its service. We do not make this information available to other users or third parties. We do not use Trip Expense Manager information or receipt images for AI analysis or any other purpose beyond providing the app's service. For our other apps, data remains on your device and is not sent to us.

Device features and permissions

Share My Location uses your location on your device when you choose to use the feature. Universal Scanner uses the camera to scan when you choose to scan; scanning is processed on your device. These features are not used for background tracking. Their data is not sent to us unless you choose to share it through another app or service.

Sharing and third parties

Trip Expense Manager transmits the expense information and receipt images you add to our servers for the app's service. We do not share that information with other users or third parties. If you choose to share information from any app using another app or service on your device, that service receives the information you send and its own privacy policy applies.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Trip Expense Manager data and receipt images are stored on our servers while you keep the corresponding data in the app on your mobile device. If you delete that data from the app on your device, the corresponding server data, including receipt images, will also be deleted. If server storage capacity needs to be freed, we may delete the oldest stored data first, including receipt images. Data in our other apps remains on your device and can be managed or removed using the app or your device's settings, including uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our apps or practices change. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.
